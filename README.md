# Banking-RAG-Assistant

## Overview

Banking-RAG-Assistant is an AI-powered Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platform designed to assist banking professionals in accessing, retrieving, and understanding information from banking documentation, regulatory guidelines, risk management frameworks, stress testing methodologies, policy documents, and operational knowledge repositories.

The platform combines Generative AI, Vector Search, Knowledge Retrieval, and Large Language Models (LLMs) to provide accurate, contextual, and explainable responses for banking use cases.

---

# Business Objective

Financial institutions manage thousands of documents covering:

- Regulatory Reporting
- Risk Analytics
- Stress Testing
- Credit Risk
- Operational Risk
- Governance Frameworks
- Internal Policies
- Audit Documentation
- Procedures & Controls

Locating relevant information manually can be time-consuming and inefficient.

The objective of this solution is to provide:

- Intelligent Document Search
- Context-Aware Responses
- Faster Knowledge Discovery
- Improved Decision Making
- Reduced Research Effort
- Enhanced Regulatory Compliance Support

---

# Business Problem Statement

Banking professionals often need answers to questions such as:

- What are the latest Risk Governance requirements?
- Which stress testing assumptions apply to a portfolio?
- What does a specific regulatory policy state?
- How should a risk metric be interpreted?
- What controls are required for a process?
- Which policy governs a particular business activity?

Traditional document repositories require manual searching.

The Banking-RAG-Assistant enables conversational access to enterprise knowledge.
